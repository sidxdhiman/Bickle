# 🗂️ Bickle

> *Your life, less chaotic.*

Bickle is a personal productivity hub — built for the way your brain actually works. Tasks, calendar, sleep schedule, and more, all in one place. No subscriptions. No bloat. Just your stuff.

---

## ✨ Features

- **📋 Task Management** — Create, track, and knock out your to-dos
- **📅 Calendar** — Visualize your days, weeks, and what's actually coming up
- **😴 Sleep Schedule** — Log and maintain healthy sleep patterns
- **🚧 More coming...** — This is a living project. Stay tuned.

---
